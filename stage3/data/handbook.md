# Nimbus Notes — Employee Handbook

Welcome to Nimbus Notes! This handbook explains how we work, what we offer, and what we expect. Nimbus Notes is a fictional company used for learning purposes. If anything here is unclear, ask your manager or email the People team.

## Working Hours

Our core hours are 10am to 4pm in your local time zone. Outside of core hours, you are free to arrange your day however works best for you, as long as you complete around 40 hours per week.

Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible. If you need to step away during core hours for an appointment, mark yourself as away in the team chat and let your manager know.

## Remote Work

Nimbus Notes is a remote-first company. You may work from home, a co-working space, or anywhere with a reliable internet connection. Working from a different country for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires approval from the People team, because of tax and legal rules.

Every new employee receives a one-time home office budget of $800. You can spend it on a desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment. Keep your receipts and submit them through the expenses tool within 60 days of your start date.

We also cover up to $50 per month for internet costs. Submit this as a recurring expense once per quarter.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays in their country of residence. Vacation days accrue monthly, at roughly 2 days per month.

You can carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next year. Any days beyond that are lost on January 1st, so plan ahead.

To request time off, submit a request in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for trips longer than three days. Shorter requests only need one week's notice. Your manager will approve or decline within three business days.

## Sick Leave

If you are unwell, you do not need to use vacation days. Simply notify your manager before your workday starts. Sick leave is unlimited for short illnesses, but absences longer than five consecutive working days require a doctor's note.

Mental health days count as sick leave. You do not need to explain the reason to anyone.

## Parental Leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birth parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken any time within the first year after a child's birth or adoption, either all at once or split into up to three blocks.

Please inform the People team at least eight weeks before you plan to start parental leave, so we can arrange cover for your work.

## Expenses and Reimbursement

Work-related expenses are reimbursed when submitted through the expenses tool with a receipt. Common examples include travel for company events, software you need for your job, and books or courses approved by your manager.

Expenses under $100 are approved automatically. Expenses over $100 need your manager's approval before you make the purchase. Reimbursements are paid with your next monthly salary, as long as they are submitted by the 20th of the month.

We do not reimburse alcohol, traffic fines, or personal entertainment.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500. You can use it for courses, conferences, certifications, or books related to your role or career growth. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

You may also spend up to four hours per week of work time on learning. Let your manager know what you are working on so they can support you.

## Equipment and Security

You will receive a company laptop on your first day. It must have full-disk encryption turned on and automatic updates enabled. Never share your password, and always use the company password manager.

If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT team within 24 hours by emailing the security inbox. The IT team can remotely lock and wipe the device.

Laptops are replaced every three years. When you leave the company, you must return your laptop within 14 days.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from two peers of your choice, and a conversation with your manager.

Salary adjustments are considered once a year, during the September review cycle. Promotions can happen in either cycle.
